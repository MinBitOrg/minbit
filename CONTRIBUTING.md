# Contributing

MinBit is built mostly by agents and reviewed by a human maintainer. Human contributions are welcome
once the contributor agreement below is in place.

- **Licence:** AGPL-3.0-or-later. By contributing you agree your work is licensed under it.
- **Sign-off:** every commit needs `Signed-off-by:` ([DCO](https://developercertificate.org/)).
- **Relicensing:** the project may later move to a more permissive licence (ADR-0014). Before accepting
  external contributions we will publish a contributor licence grant that allows this; PRs from
  external contributors are on hold until then.
- **Process:** open an issue first, follow [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) conventions (branch naming, Conventional
  Commits, escalation labels).
